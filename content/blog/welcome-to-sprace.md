Creator marketing in the Nordics is growing fast — but the tooling has lagged behind. Briefs still live in DMs, payments still run through invoices, and disputes tend to end with someone feeling burned.

We built Sprace because we wanted one place where a brief becomes a booking, a booking becomes a delivery, and a delivery becomes a paid collaboration — without anyone chasing a PDF at the end of the month.

## What we care about

We care about three things, in this order.

1. **Creators get paid.** Every booking is funded upfront through Stripe. The money sits in escrow while you work, and it lands in your account when the business approves the delivery. No chasing invoices.
2. **Businesses get what they asked for.** Every brief is contract-shaped. Scope, deadline, budget and deliverables are written down before anyone starts working, and there is a clear path to revisions or a refund if things go sideways.
3. **The marketplace is boring in the best way.** Taxes, GDPR, receipts, dispute mediation — we handle the unglamorous parts so the creative work can stay creative.

## Who it is for

Sprace is not for every collaboration. It is built for:

- **UGC creators** who want to turn weekend work into consistent income without becoming a part-time accountant.
- **Influencers and content creators** in the Nordics who want to work with brands that actually understand their niche.
- **Marketing teams at small and mid-sized brands** who want to run creator campaigns without building a procurement process around it.

If that sounds like you, [create an account](/register) and take a look around. It is free to sign up and free to publish a profile.

## What is next

We will use this blog to share updates that matter — launch notes, small how-tos, and the occasional opinion piece about the state of creator marketing. No growth-hacking lists, no engagement bait.

Welcome to Sprace. We are glad you are here.
